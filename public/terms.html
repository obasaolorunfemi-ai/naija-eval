Terms of use

Last updated 3 November 2026

In short: Naija Eval is independent research, published free. It is a first pass on 100 items per language and direction, not a certification. Quote it and cite it, but test models on your own data before you rely on them.

1. About these terms

These terms apply when you use naija-eval.extrafemi.com, published by Extrafemi Labs. By using the site you agree to them. If you do not agree, please do not use it.

2. This is research, not advice

Naija Eval reports how models scored on public test sets under the method described on the page. It is not legal, procurement, investment or policy advice, and it is not an endorsement or a criticism of any organisation. Results may change as we test more data, more models or newer versions.

3. Limits of the results

The method section of the report lists the limits, including sample size, 4-bit quantization of the local models, possible training-data contamination, and the gap between clean read speech and real voice notes. Scores describe these tests only. Do not use them alone for decisions about safety, health, law, money or people's rights.
