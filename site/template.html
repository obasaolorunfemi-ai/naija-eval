What we found7 findings

Findings

  1. The fine-tune is real. N-ATLaS beats its Llama 3.1 base by chrF++ on average. English to Yoruba goes from 7.6 to 24.5.
  2. It writes Hausa close to the best. English to Hausa scores 47.4, against 51.7 for the top hosted model.
  3. It is weakest translating into English. Hausa to English scores 51.2 against 67.2; Yoruba to English 37.6 against 57.1.
  4. Yoruba is hard for every model. No model passes 33 for English to Yoruba, and ignoring tone marks lifts every model by about 10 points.
  5. The cheapest hosted model still wins on quality. Claude Haiku 5.5 beat N-ATLaS in all six directions; its 600 translations cost $0.03.
  6. N-ATLaS's advantage is where it runs. It runs offline on a laptop or a server in Nigeria, with no text or audio leaving the machine.
  7. One correction to the model card. N-ATLaS is built on Llama 3.1 8B (its config uses Llama 3.1's rope scaling and 128k positions), not Llama 3.
